## Part 1 — Slide-by-Slide Speaker Notes

### Slide 1 — Cover
Open with the one-line thesis: W+ is an AI-native social platform — not a social app with AI added later, but a platform where content, communication and intelligence were designed as one ecosystem from day one. Keep this slide to 20 seconds — establish category, stage, and that the product is real and shipping.

### Slide 2 — The Problem
Frame the problem in three cuts. Fragmentation: a typical user runs one app for messaging, another for calls, another for short video, another for communities — identity and social graph are scattered. Incentives: incumbent feeds are advertising machines first; genuine communication is subordinated to impression maximization. Architecture: the giants are retrofitting AI onto systems designed 10–20 years ago, so AI stays a feature, not a foundation. Land the closing line: nobody owns the complete loop of create → communicate → discover. That gap is the opportunity.

### Slide 3 — The Solution
Walk the three pillars left to right. Connect: full messenger with text, photo and video, plus voice and video calls — communication is native, not an afterthought. Create: video profiles as the identity primitive, short videos and daily moments, plus creator and community tools. Discover: AI recommendations, nearby people, global communities — discovery driven by relevance to the person, not to an advertiser. Then the bottom band, the strategic point: AI is not a chatbot bolted to the side; it is the connective tissue between all three pillars. That is what "AI-native" means operationally.
